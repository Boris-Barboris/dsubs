Aesthetics:
    high cost, high fidelity, this took a lot of time and tuning
    VFX deserves the highest praise: engines, smoke, explosions, nuke flash,
        animaiton/camera sync.
    SFX honestly top notch.
    rain effect is impressive first 20 times.
    nice g-lock blackout flavor effect. Magazine animation is nice and punchy.
    Shooting is satisfying.
Gameplay:
    UI problems:
        UI is style over usability, obstructs and annoys.
        UI is fixed-aspect-ratio due to stylization.
        Glitch shaders over the top.
        WASD-only camera panning
        zoom too coarse-grained, annoying glitches
        fucking RAIN EFFECT
    game world multilayering (old problem):
        strategic layer, tactical layer, dock layer
            jumping around is annoying
        tactically you are as if strategic layer no longer exists, isolation
        time acceleration as an obvious consequence
        aircraft cannnot be uysed in tactical layer. arbitrary barriers.
    physics simulation are too heavy to keep tactical layers real-time on strategy map
    useless radio-intercept mini-game
    graphics/drawing tools are primitive, obviously elint needs advanced TMA
    radar UI annoying, why can't it be overlayed over the tacmap?
    fleet split/merge unreliable
    refueling cannot be ordered from the strategic map
    unintuitive inistant and obscure fuel split system
    annoying landing mini-game that is fun only first 15 times
    useless RPG dialogue mechanics of persuasion, no depth and only little flavor.
    while the tutorial is good, it is not enough as it does not teach surviving in the campaign (mainly, the economy is unexplained).
    difficulty curve fine for good players but brutal for genre newcommers.
