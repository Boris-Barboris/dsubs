     Blindness: Adds a layer of tension and unpredictability.  Combat, negotiations, even simple interactions become more dangerous.

     Memorable & Unique: It's not a typical "underwater" or "combat" title. It stands out and sticks in the mind.

Overall, "Blind Saloon: Under a Frozen Sky" is a fantastic title that captures the essence of your game in a creative, evocative, and memorable way.  It has a great mix of intrigue, atmosphere, and thematic depth.
